Report a sighting

Only submit a photo you took yourself. Group photos are fine: other people in it are never shown to families (they are blurred) and their face data is deleted once the photo is matched. It is compared only against verified missing-person cases. Matches are checked by a trained reviewer before anything is shared with a family. Unmatched photos are deleted after 30 days.

I'm safe — don't locate me

If you are the person in a case and you are safe, enter the case code (e.g. from a poster). A reviewer confirms it, the case is closed, and nothing about you — including any sightings — is shared. While it is reviewed, nothing is released.
